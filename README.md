## About

I'm a Full Stack Developer based in Dakar, Senegal, with 4+ years of experience turning business problems into digital products — from UX/UI design to production deployment.

In 2024, I founded **[Kamit Digital Solutions](https://kamit.tech)** to support African startups in their digital transformation, providing tailored web, mobile and AI-powered solutions adapted to each client's needs and budget. My work spans web applications, mobile apps (React Native, Flutter), business software and branding — end to end, from strategy to code.
